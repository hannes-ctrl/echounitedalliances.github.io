# Privacy Policy — Resonance accounts

*Last updated 9 September 2026.*

This page says exactly what a **Resonance** account collects, why, and who
sees it — all of it checkable against the code that runs this site. It
covers the account system specifically; the alliance's general privacy
practices (the visitor counter, the Discord widget, browser storage that
has nothing to do with an account) are summarised at the end so this page
isn't misleading on its own, but they're also covered on the site's own
[Privacy page](/#/privacy).

We're a hobby group, not a company, and we collect as little as we can get
away with.

## What you give us at signup

Creating a Resonance account asks for two things: an email address and a
password. Nothing else is required.

Your password never touches this site's own code or storage. It's sent
over TLS straight to Supabase — the service that hosts this site's
database and handles sign-in — which stores it hashed. Nobody running this
site can see it.

## What you can add afterward

All optional, and all editable or removable at any time from the Resonance
page:

- a display name (defaults to the part of your email before the `@` if you
  never set one);
- a home division;
- a home airport.

## What gets stored when you book a trip

A booking made while signed in is tied to your account, and includes:

- a contact name and email address (defaults to your account's, editable
  per booking);
- the passengers' given and family names, as you typed them — these are
  names for a fictional booking, not verified identity, but they're stored
  as you entered them;
- the route, dates, fare, and cabin for each segment.

This is fictional travel inside a game: no real payment is taken, and
nothing here is verified against a real identity document.

## Who else sees it

Two services are involved in running the account system, and each has its
own privacy policy:

- **Supabase**, which hosts the database, stores your bookings and profile,
  and handles sign-in.
- **GitHub Pages**, which serves this site and records ordinary web request
  logs, the same as any static website host.

We don't sell your data, share it with advertisers, or pass it to anyone
outside the alliance. There's nothing here worth selling, and no one is
trying to.

## Email

The only email a Resonance account can generate is a password reset you
asked for. We don't send marketing email, and we don't email you for any
other reason.

## What your browser stores

Signing in keeps a session in your browser's own storage, on your device,
so you aren't asked to sign in on every page. It contains no personal data
beyond what's needed to keep you signed in, and it's cleared when you sign
out.

The site also keeps a copy of public alliance figures it has already shown
you — the division totals, the network map and the headline counts — so
the next visit opens on them instead of waiting for the database. That copy
is the same for every visitor and says nothing about you.

## Children

This site and the alliance are not intended for anyone under 13, matching
Discord's own minimum age. If you are under 13, please do not create an
account. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, tell a
board member and we will remove it.

## Getting your data removed

Open a ticket in the Discord server, or send a direct message to any board
member or division leader, and say what you want removed. We will delete
your Resonance account, the bookings attached to it, or both. You don't
have to give a reason.

## The rest of the site, briefly

Two things happen on this site whether or not you have an account, and
neither identifies you:

- a live count of how many browser tabs currently have the site open, each
  joining with a random identifier generated fresh every visit and
  discarded when you leave;
- a count of members currently online in our Discord server, read from
  Discord's own public widget.

Neither is stored anywhere, and neither is tied to a Resonance account.
Full detail is on the site's own [Privacy page](/#/privacy).

## Changes

If what we collect changes, this page changes in the same commit as the
code that changed. The date at the top is when that last happened.

## Questions

Open a ticket in the Discord server, or send a direct message to any board
member or division leader.
